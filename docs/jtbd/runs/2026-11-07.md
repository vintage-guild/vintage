**Step by step:**
- **Wrong surname:** `armory-link` with `Ill` answered pending, "fetching the armory now". About 6 seconds later the scheduled `armory:refresh` had run against Blizzard, and `armory-get` showed `not-found` with no error.
- **Right surname, at once:** `armory-link` with `Il` answered pending, with "the armory was asked moments ago, so it's fetched within the hour (or armory-refresh in 10 minutes)". No fetch ran. `armory-refresh` answered `ARMORY_REFRESH_TOO_SOON`, "Try again in 10 minutes".
- **The sweep:** after 10 minutes, `armory:sweep` answered `requested: 1`. Seconds later `armory-get` showed `found`: level 20 Warrior, Orc, Horde, guild Vintage (`vintage`), Protection, equipped item level 13, 13 items (the first was Brawler's Harness, common, item level 1), a portrait at `render.worldofwarcraft.com/forever-us/…-avatar.jpg`, and `classMismatch` false against the registered warrior.
- **Who sees what:** Pip (member) got `CHARACTER_NOT_FOUND` from `armory-get` and `armory-link` on Bloodfen's character, and `FORBIDDEN` from `armory-list allGuild:true`. Pip's `armory-record` got `COMMAND_NOT_FOUND`. Bloodfen (raider) listed the guild's one link.
- **Unlink:** `armory-unlink` previewed `bloodfen-il`; confirming with the token removed it. `armory-get` showed no link, and `character-list` still had Bloodfen.

## Observations

- **No active spec in the profile.** Forever's profile has no `active_spec`, but `/specializations` lists the active group's talent trees with `spent_points`. `fetchArmory` now takes the tree with the most points; the live character came back Protection.
- **Every endpoint answered on US** with client credentials: profile, equipment, media, specializations, statistics, professions, status, raid encounters (empty before Dec 9), and the guild roster (91 members, each with a surname and slug). An unknown character 404s everywhere. Details in `libs/forever-api/README.md`.
- **The probe** (`apps/api/scripts/armory-probe.ts`) prints each endpoint's status and shape, then the module's own fetch, without the token or secret.
