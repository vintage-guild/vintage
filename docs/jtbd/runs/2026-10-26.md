**J17, step by step:**
- **Create:** `/vintage poll create` (three options, defaults) answered privately with "Your poll is going up in this channel. It closes in a day." The card posted in `#general` with one button per option, "Take back my vote", and "Close poll".
- **Vote:** clicking **Thursday** answered privately with "You voted for **Thursday**". The post was edited to Thursday 1 · 100%, "1 of 1 voted".
- **Change:** clicking **Tuesday** moved the vote, still one ballot, and the post followed.
- **Close preview:** **Close poll** showed a private preview, "Tuesday wins with 1 ballot. Closing can't be undone.", with a **Close it** button. The poll stayed open.
- **Confirm:** **Close it** closed the poll. Production data shows `status: closed`, the winner frozen as Tuesday, and the post's last render key `1|closed|1|1|1,0,0`. The final card then showed in Discord: marked closed, Tuesday ✓ at 1 · 100%, and no buttons, with the private reply "Closed … Tuesday wins with 1 ballot."
- **Not run live:** the Member refusal, hidden and anonymous results, a non-creator trying to close, and the pick-any menu. They need more test accounts.

## Observations

- **Before the deploy, every poll failed.** Production Convex and the gateway were still on #75, so the gateway didn't forward `/vintage poll`, and Discord showed "Vintage didn't respond in time". Both deploys are manual (SETUP, Deployments), and the project's `.claude/settings.json` denies `npx convex deploy` to agents.
- **Results were slow to show.** Each vote or close took about 10–15 seconds to show its result in Discord. Discord never reported a timeout, so the interaction was answered in time. The cause wasn't measured. It could be cold starts on the gateway or Convex, or the Discord client. Worth timing before raid nights depend on it.
- **The register script's error is misleading.** `register-commands` reads only process env, and its error points to an `apps/gateway/.env.example` that doesn't exist.
